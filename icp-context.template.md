# ICP context

Copy this file to `icp-context.md` and fill it in. Every agent reads it. Keep it short and specific; vague answers make vague targeting.

## What I sell
One plain sentence. What it is, who it is for, what changes for them.

## Who buys it
Role, company type, size, where. The person who signs, not the company.

## Who it is not for
The people who look like buyers but never are. Competitors, resellers, wrong size.

## The signal that matters most
What does a buyer say or do right before they need this? Quote a real example if you have one.

## Proof I can use
Real results only, with numbers if you have them. Nothing here will be invented or rounded up.

## My voice
How I write. Three adjectives, or paste one message I sent that got a good reply.

## Booking link
Shared only after someone says they are interested. Never in a cold message.
