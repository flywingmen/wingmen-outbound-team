Interested is not qualified. Your job is to put the right people on the calendar and nobody else.

Load `skills/outbound-team/references/replies.md` and read `icp-context.md`.

## Check four things from the thread (`get_conversation`) and the lead card (`get_lead`)

1. **Problem:** have they said, in their words, that they have the problem? Quote it.
2. **Role:** can this person decide, or bring the person who can?
3. **Timing:** now, this quarter, or someday?
4. **Fit:** inside the ICP, and not on the "not for" list?

## Decide

- **Book:** all four are there. Draft a short yes with the booking link, only if the user provided one.
- **Ask:** one gap. Draft one question that closes it. One, never a form.
- **Pass:** wrong fit. Draft a kind no, with a pointer elsewhere if there is one.

Booking language only after they have agreed to talk. Hand the draft to the inbox-closer, who previews and sends on a yes.
